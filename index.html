HOW THIS WORKS

Squeektoshi runs on a real wheel. Every accepted pass of the magnet is one work unit, and work is counted in rounds of ten minutes. This arcade is those rules with the clock taken out: the numbers are his, the round is yours to close.

  1. A round holds 10,000 work units. That is not a game limit, it is the paper's: ten minutes at the minimum 60 ms between passes is exactly 10,000. Anything past it is rejected, so the wheel stops counting rather than overflow.
  2. His work arrives in bursts. A burst is a stretch of continuous running, so it carries a work count. A round offers twelve of them, and they add up to 13,000 work against 10,000 of room: about three bursts a round have to go, and choosing which is the game. You take a burst into the round or you let it pass. There is no third option and no undo, because work never moves from one round to another.
  3. Fill the round, do not overflow it. A burst that does not fit is rejected, and it still leaves the chute. Room left when the last burst is handled is lost the same way.
  4. The round is priced at the difficulty. Difficulty is the work that 1,000 Squeek blocks cost. Your work is converted at that price, and every billion block units completes one Squeek block.
  5. You move the price yourself. After a round with at least 40 work, difficulty steps one thirtieth of the way toward the level where that round's work would have earned exactly 40 blocks. Pack a heavy round and every round after it pays less per unit. That is the ratchet, not a penalty invented here.
  6. Four rounds, one shift. The block the page is reading seeds the burst stream, so the same block is the same shift for everyone. The burst sitting in the chute before you press START is that shift's first burst, handed over rather than drawn again, so it is the one you get. SHIFT SEED is the first four hex of that seed.
  7. You can hear it and you can check it. Tones are generated in the browser, not loaded from a file. Every number on this page can be recomputed from the formulas below with a calculator.
  8. Holders get sight, not score. Paste a Squeek address or a member code under HOLDER and the chute shows you the next burst before it arrives. It changes nothing on chain and adds nothing to the score, because a tier is not a multiplier in the whitepaper and it is not one here.
The four expressions this game runs on, quoted from the whitepaper
blockUnits += floor(w × 10¹² / D)a round's work, priced at the difficulty in force
D_next = floor((29 × D + floor(w × 1,000 / 40)) / 30)when the round had at least 40 work
D_next = Dwhen it had less, difficulty does not move
height = floor(blockUnits / 10⁹)every billion block units completes one block

When the API is silent the difficulty falls back to SqueekCore.state() at 0xFD6C166D4B8E2962E65746a13e5a0797CD4dE0c4 on Sepolia, read with one eth_call over a public RPC. If that is silent too, the shift starts at the paper's floor of 1,000 and says so on screen rather than inventing a number. Nothing here signs, spends, or asks for a wallet.

Scores are kept in this browser only.

HOLDER SHIFT

The arcade is free for everyone and stays that way. If your Squeekchain profile holds something, paste it and the chute shows the next burst before it arrives: one burst of sight, so you can decide whether the room you have left is worth spending. It adds nothing to your score, because nothing in the whitepaper makes a Squeek worth more work.

Open shift: no sight.

What a profile is worth, and what each tier shows you
1per Squeek in the wallet, per holder collection, per builder badge
2per staked Squeek, because staking is the half that does the work
3per one-of-one trophy (First Squeek, First Invitooor, First Buildooooor)
LIGHTweight 1 to 2: one burst of sight
HEAVYweight 3 to 5: one burst of sight, tier stamped on the card
PRIMEweight 6 or more: one burst of sight, tier stamped on the card

An address is read through api.squeekchain.com/v1/accounts/. A member code is read through community.squeekchain.com/v1/community/profiles/, which only sends CORS headers to squeekchain.com, so api.php in this folder fetches it server-side and hands back nothing but the badge ids, the Aura and the rank. Neither read is signed, so neither can prove the profile is yours. That is on purpose: sight is a courtesy, not a claim. Nothing is stored on the server, and the tier stays in this browser.
